PHOTOGRAPHS FOR THE TRAININGS SHELF
===================================

One folder per programme, named exactly as the programme is:

    incoming/Trainings/Ninja/
    incoming/Trainings/Ninja Plus/
    incoming/Trainings/Ninja Pro/
    incoming/Trainings/AI Ready Engineer/
    incoming/Trainings/Industry Readiness Program/
    incoming/Trainings/LaunchPad/
    incoming/Trainings/Skill Sprint/

Drop JPG or PNG files in. They are shown, at their own proportions, under that
programme's open page -- the one you reach by pressing its book -- and the page
scrolls down to them. A programme with an empty folder has no photographs and
its page does not scroll.

Then:   node tools/publish-trainings.cjs
