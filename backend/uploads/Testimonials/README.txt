=====================

  anjana.jpg

Portrait crop, at least 800px on the short edge. They are drawn as tall arches,
so a head-and-shoulders shot with the face in the upper half works best.

WHY NOT PULL THEM FROM THE FILMS: both of YouTube's thumbnail routes were tried
- the custom thumbnail and the auto-generated frames - and every one is the
video's designed title card with the student's name burned across it in green.
This deck does not place a raw poster; the page's own typography carries the
name. Until a photo arrives each student shows their initials on a coloured
arch, which is honest and looks deliberate.
