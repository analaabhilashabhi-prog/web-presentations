SUCCESS STORIES — the portraits
===============================

Drop the photos straight into this folder. Portrait crops work best; the wall
draws them at roughly 3:4 and the opened view shows the whole picture, uncropped.

  backend/uploads/stories/priya.jpg
  backend/uploads/stories/arun.jpg

Filenames do not matter — they order alphabetically, so number them if you want
a particular order: 01-..., 02-...

RESOLUTION: at least 800px on the short edge. The opened portrait fills three
quarters of the screen, so a 206px thumbnail will look soft on a projector.

WORDS (optional) — content.txt in this folder, one block per photo:

  photo: priya.jpg
  name: Priya R
  role: Cognizant · 2026 batch
  quote: The mock interviews were the turning point.
  body: Joined the Owl Coder track in her third year and finished the
        placement practice before campus season opened.
  ---
  photo: arun.jpg
  name: Arun K
  role: Red Hat Academy · certified
  ---

A photo with no entry still appears on the wall; it opens without words beside
it. Nothing is invented — if you do not write a name, none is shown.

Tell me when the photos are in and I will re-run the publish step.
